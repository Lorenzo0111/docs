# Hitboxes

Since version 2.6, every vehicle has a hitbox that matches its shape:

- Click anywhere on a vehicle to enter it.
- Click near a passenger seat to sit in that seat.
- Hit the vehicle anywhere to damage it, if `enable_VehicleDamage` is on.
- Players riding a vehicle no longer collide with it.

On 1.19.4 and newer, the plugin places invisible Interaction entities over the hitbox. On older versions it checks where the player is looking instead. You can change this in the [`hitboxes`](/qualityarmoryvehicles/configuration#hitboxes) section of the main config.

## Generated hitboxes

If a vehicle file has no `hitbox` option, the plugin generates one from:

- the driver and passenger seats, with some space around them and room above for the riders
- the vehicle length, from `center`, `front_vector_offset` and `back_vector_offset`
- the legacy `widthOffset` and `heightOffset` options

This works for most vehicles. If the generated hitbox does not match the model, set the `hitbox` option.

## The hitbox option

The `hitbox` option is one box, or a list of boxes for vehicles with a more complex shape.

| Key | Description | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `length` | Size along the front axis (X) | Required |
| `width` | Size along the side axis (Z) | Required |
| `height` | Size along the vertical axis (Y) | Required |
| `x` | X of the center of the box | `0` |
| `y` | Y of the bottom of the box | `0` |
| `z` | Z of the center of the box | `0` |

The positions are relative to the vehicle model: +X points to the front of the vehicle, +Y is up and +Z is to the side. `length`, `width` and `height` must be greater than 0, or the box is skipped and a warning is shown in the console.

### One box

```yaml
hitbox:
  length: 4.5
  width: 2.2
  height: 1.8
```

### Several boxes

For example, a truck with a tall cab and a lower bed:

```yaml
hitbox:
  - length: 2.0
    width: 2.4
    height: 2.8
    x: 1.8
  - length: 3.5
    width: 2.4
    height: 1.6
    x: -1.0
```

## Seeing the hitboxes

Use `/qav hitbox` to show the hitboxes and seats of nearby vehicles with particles. Run it again to hide them.

If you are looking at a vehicle when you run the command, the chat shows:

- the boxes of that vehicle, and whether they were generated
- the point you are looking at, in the vehicle's local coordinates
- the seat that a click on that point would select

Use the local coordinates to place the `x`, `y` and `z` of your boxes.

<Info>
The command needs the `qualityarmoryvehicles.debug` permission.
</Info>
